The fragrance

Quiet depth.
Lasting presence.

Santal Obscur explores wood in its most intimate form. The fragrance opens with the subtle brightness of bergamot and cardamom before settling into a textured heart of sandalwood and cedar.

As it develops, vetiver, amber and soft musk emerge close to the skin, leaving behind a warm and quietly distinctive trail.

Grounded without feeling heavy. Refined without demanding attention.
